# Kognitywistyka — treatment (Watercolor Brush, 30 s)

Brief: 30 s, 1920×1080, 60 fps (style), English narrator (Kokoro), Polish subtitles burned in + `.srt`, on-screen notes in Polish, ending with PJATK (Polsko-Japońska Akademia Technik Komputerowych). No storyboard review.

## 1. Three structures

A. **Transect**: a long lateral dolly across one painted landscape where each discipline is a region, ending at a city = mind. Strong but needs 60+ s to read five regions.
B. **Specimen plate / field journal**: one locked journal sheet. A hand-written question opens it; a head in profile is painted as the "specimen"; five disciplines are painted around it as small specimens with notes; their pigments then run into the head and mix. Pull back to the whole page, signed with a PJATK stamp.
C. **Recipe**: a tall sheet tilting down, each discipline an "ingredient", the mind the dish. Cute, but makes the mind a product.

**Chosen: B.** It fits 30 s (one sheet, no cuts), the specimen + note grammar is the style's core, and the turn ("no single science answers alone" → the colours mix into one mind) is a native move: *palette as data / pigment bleeding*. Opening from the variation list: *a hand-written question on blank paper, answered by painting*. Ending: *pull back to the whole page; the brush lifts; a vermilion stamp*.

## 2. Logline and arc

A question written on blank paper — how does a mind work? — is answered not by one science but by five, whose colours run together into one painted mind.

- Setup (0–7 s): the question; a head profile paints itself; "no single science can answer alone".
- Development (7–19 s): five disciplines appear as specimens with notes, each its own pigment.
- Turn (19–24 s): brush lifted (near-silence), then the pigments bleed along painted paths into the head and blend.
- Ending (24–30 s): pull back; title "Kognitywistyka", note "Polsko-Japońska Akademia Technik Komputerowych", vermilion stamp PJATK.

## 3. Benchmark

Naturalist field-journal plates and anatomical "phrenology"-style teaching charts (grammar only).
- Learn: one hero specimen per sheet, labels on leader lines, generous margins, structure-before-detail.
- Don't take: any specific plate, the phrenology zones themselves (pseudo-science), any layout.

## 6. Cue map

Tempo 84 bpm (beat 0.714 s), 4/4. Felt-piano-like plucked kalimba/glockenspiel + soft pad (synthesised: no generic strings).
- Bars 1–2 (0–5.7): solo kalimba motif, sparse.
- Bars 3–6 (5.7–17.1): motif + soft pad; each specimen lands on a downbeat-ish onset (7.4, 9.8, 12.8, 14.8, 16.8 → foley brush on each).
- 19.2–20.4: silence (brush lifted).
- 20.4–24.6: pad swells, motif in octaves; pigment-bleed swells.
- 24.6–30: resolve chord; stamp thump at 27.6; tail fades to paper.
